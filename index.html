Purpose and permissions

Understand what you connect

How Google services are used

Google sign-in can identify your account. An optional YouTube connection supports the YouTube features you authorize, such as subscribed content, channel analytics, or supported actions. Requested permissions are shown before access is granted.

Google account data is used to provide those features. It is not used to train general-purpose AI models or to target advertising. Read how data is accessed, stored, shared and deleted.

Safety is part of using UFIConnect

Non-consensual intimate imagery, including AI-generated content, is prohibited. UFIConnect does not use Google APIs to create or enable AI-generated non-consensual intimate imagery.

Report a post through its menu or use our copyright reporting process. Community guidelines also cover harassment, exploitation, impersonation and other abuse.

Your privacy choices

Optional analytics and homepage ad measurement have separate consent controls. You can review or withdraw consent, disconnect supported accounts, and request access to or deletion of your information.

Questions

Good to know

Is UFIconnect free?

Yes. The core feed, profile, communities, messaging, and opportunities are free to start.

Can I browse without creating an account?

Yes. Choose Explore the feed to browse public posts before creating an account.

Where does the feed content come from?

The feed includes UFIConnect member posts and content from available external sources. Source names and original links remain visible. Connections, playback, and actions depend on each provider’s permissions and availability.

Is Resume Builder available?

Yes. Choose $30/month or $149.94 every 6 months. Both options include the same Resume Builder tools.
