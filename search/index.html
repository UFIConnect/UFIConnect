Find posts, people, groups and jobs

Search brings the different parts of UFIconnect together. Sign in to search member profiles, discover communities, find posts by topic, and explore job opportunities.

Start with what you want to find

  • Posts: try an interest, subject, or keyword, then choose the sources that matter to you.
  • People: look for a name, shared interest, or professional connection.
  • Groups: find a community around a topic and review its description before joining.
  • Jobs: use a role, skill, or location to narrow the opportunities you explore.

Use a short, specific phrase first. If there are no useful results, try a broader term or adjust your filters. Search results depend on the content available and your account access.
