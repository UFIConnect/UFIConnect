## Approve a retention schedule

For every data class, record: purpose, system/table/vendor, retention trigger and duration, legal/accounting exception, deletion mechanism, backup aging and post-restore deletion behavior, owner, and last test. Do not copy a suggested 30/90-day number into the public policy without an implemented mechanism or verified provider setting.

A privacy owner should reconcile notices with this inventory and assess US state scope and EU obligations from the business facts in [README](README.md). Include minors and sensitive data: COPPA can apply to child-directed services and general-audience services with actual knowledge of collecting personal information from under-13 children. An age statement alone does not demonstrate handling of actual knowledge. [FTC COPPA guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions)
