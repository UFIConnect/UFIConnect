Platform overview

How UFI works

A shared workspace for content discovery, communities and professional activity.

UFIConnect organizes available content from multiple sources into mixed, source-specific and following feeds. Source attribution, account permissions and provider playback rules remain attached to the content throughout that process.

From the source to your screen

How content reaches your feed

Public sources and authorized account connections follow separate access rules. UFIConnect brings the available results into a consistent reading interface.

  1. 01

    Source access

    Public feeds supply content through available source integrations. Account connections require your authorization and the provider’s supported permissions.

  2. 02

    Content organization

    Posts are checked and organized with their author, source, timestamp, text and available media. Background refreshes update public content; availability and freshness vary by source.

  3. 03

    Feed selection

    Your chosen view, source filters, topics and account scope determine which posts are eligible. Your preferences and sort controls shape the results you browse.

  4. 04

    Reading and actions

    Read the available post, play supported media, save content or open the original. Reporting is available on posts; external interactions depend on the provider.

Feed controls

Mixed feeds, source feeds and Following

Mixed feed

The mixed feed combines UFIConnect posts and available external content. Your saved interests guide discovery. Explicit source and topic filters let you narrow the results, while the selected sort order controls their presentation.

Visitors can browse the public Global Feed. Sign in to personalize your home feed, use source filters and access Following. Clearing an explicit source selection returns to the broader feed allowed by that view.

Connected features

How the workspace fits together

Feed, Videos and Music

Different views of available content. Media uses supported playback routes, with links to the original source when playback belongs on the provider’s service.

People, groups and messages

Your UFIConnect identity connects your profile, community participation and conversations. Following helps you return to relevant accounts and communities.

Opportunities and resume tools

Career listings connect discovery with role information and the original application page. Resume tools support preparation and applications.

Business recruiting

A Business membership provides a workspace focused on work and recruiting. Membership and team settings manage access to those tools.

Vault and the planned device

The browser Vault stores encrypted personal items locally. The planned device extends the concept to authenticated, automated sign-ins.

Account and privacy controls

Settings brings together account security, appearance, connected services and membership management. You control which supported accounts to connect.

Permissions and availability

What stays with the source

One interface, separate service permissions

Connecting a service authorizes only the supported access you grant. It does not sign you into every provider, transfer ownership of their content or make every external action available inside UFIConnect.

Deleted content, access changes, rate limits and unavailable media can affect what appears. Original links and source identity remain visible so you can inspect the context at the source.
